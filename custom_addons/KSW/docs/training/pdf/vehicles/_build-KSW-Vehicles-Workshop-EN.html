The Workshop Manager's Queue, Sites and Handover Forms

Who is this for: Workshop Managers and Technicians How long it takes: ongoing

What this does

There are three workshops: Nabiya (the main one), Tabuk and Al-Hayat. Every request goes to one of them, and you work the queue of your own workshop(s). Besides repair requests, the workshop has a step on the vehicle handover forms (HR-12): inspecting a vehicle that is handed in, and handing a vehicle over to its new department.

Before you start

  • You are in your workshop's Managers & Staff (Workshop → Configuration → Workshops). In none, you see nothing: ask your administrator.
  • The GM and the reviewers see every workshop; on the dashboard they switch between them.

Where a request goes

A request goes to the workshop that serves the requester's department (or its nearest parent department), otherwise to Nabiya. Requests raised before the three workshops existed are Nabiya's.

  1. Open Workshop → All Requests. The Workshop column shows the site; you only see yours.

  2. Work the requests as before: accept (In Progress), fill the repair report, Complete or Reject. See the requester's guide, Issuing a workshop service request, for what they see.

Handover forms (HR-12)

Open them from your Home page (Workshop: TRN-…) or Vehicle Custody → Handover Forms (HR-12).

Form Your part
Hand In (Release) inspect the vehicle that is handed in, in parallel with HR's documents check. A checkup Workshop Request is opened with it (button at the top)
Receive after HR has checked the new driver, hand the vehicle over to him: that is its arrival in the new department

  1. Open the Vehicle Inspection tab. Fill Inspected On and, per item (clutch, tyres, brakes…), the Depreciation %, Value and a remark. The Total Value is what Accounting receives.

  2. Click Workshop Done. On a hand-in the form is Approved when HR has also checked the documents; on a receipt it is the arrival: the vehicle is the department's and the driver's from now on.

A driver change between two drivers of the same department has no workshop step any more.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
The queue is empty Your user has no workshop Ask the administrator to add you to your workshop
A request went to the wrong workshop The requester's department is served by another workshop, or by none (then Nabiya) The administrator sets Departments Served under Workshop → Configuration → Workshops
No Workshop Done button on a Receive HR has not checked the driver yet It comes after Documents Checked
