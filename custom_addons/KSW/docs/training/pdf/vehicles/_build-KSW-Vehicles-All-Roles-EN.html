Releasing and Requesting Vehicles

Who is this for: Department supervisors who answer for vehicles (Vehicle Allocation, Department User) How long it takes: 3 minutes per request

What this does

Every company vehicle belongs to a department. When your department no longer needs a vehicle you release it; when it needs more you request them. Both are only asks: the General Manager decides each vehicle. You never move a vehicle yourself, and no vehicle leaves another department unless that department released it.

Request You say The GM can
Release Vehicles which vehicles you give up, why, and whether the driver goes with it give it to a department that asked, send it to the workshop to hold, or keep it with you
Request Vehicles how many of which type, and whether you need the driver too give released or pooled vehicles, obtain them outside (buy/rent), or refuse

Before you start

  • You need Vehicle Allocation → Department User for your department(s). If the Vehicle Allocation app is missing, ask your system administrator.
  • You only see and release your own department's vehicles.

Releasing vehicles

  1. Open Vehicle Allocation → Requests → Release Vehicles. The list shows your releases and their state.

  2. Click New. Department is filled in (pick it if you answer for more than one). Write the reason in the box under it.

  3. Add each vehicle under Vehicles to Release: the Vehicle, and in Why a short reason for that one. Tick With Driver only when the driver moves with the vehicle to the department that gets it (he keeps it in his custody and is transferred with it).

  4. Click Submit. The request moves to Waiting for GM and the GM gets it on his Home page.

Requesting vehicles

  1. Open Vehicle Allocation → Requests → Request Vehicles and click New.

  2. Add a line per vehicle type under Vehicles Needed: the Vehicle Type, Needs, Quantity and What For:

    Needs Choose it when
    Vehicle Only you have a driver for it
    Vehicle with Driver you need the driver too
    Either both are fine

  3. Click Submit.

What happens next

  • The GM decides each vehicle. The request stays Waiting for GM until every line is decided, then it is Closed. Each line shows its own Status (Transfer, To Workshop / Holding, Kept by Department…).
  • A vehicle that leaves you goes to the workshop with a Hand In (Release) handover form (HR-12): the workshop inspects it and HR checks its papers. Its custodian is cleared when both are done.
  • A vehicle that comes to you arrives with a Receive form. On your Home page you get Name the new driver: open the form, choose the New User, and click Submit to HR. HR checks his licence, then the workshop hands the vehicle over, which is its arrival in your department. The checks in Changing a vehicle's driver apply to that driver too.
  • With Driver: no workshop and no form; the vehicle and its driver move together the day the GM decides, and the driver's transfer is applied.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
A vehicle is not in the list when releasing It is not your department's, or it is already on another release or being moved Check Vehicle Allocation → Vehicles; a vehicle on its way finishes moving first
I cannot edit my request It has been submitted Write in the chatter; the GM can return it to draft
"Waiting for GM" for days The GM has not decided yet It is on his Home page; ask him

Changing a Vehicle's Driver (HR-12)

Who is this for: Department supervisors (Vehicle Allocation, Department User) How long it takes: 2 minutes

What this does

Hands one of your vehicles from its current driver to another driver of your own department: vacation, suspension, a new route. It is your department's own decision: there is no GM step, no workshop checkup and no HR step. When every check passes, Confirm Driver Change gives the vehicle to the new driver at once, and HR and Accounting are told.

Before you start

The system checks these before the vehicle changes hands, and the New User list only offers drivers who pass the first four:

Check Why
One driver, one vehicle: he does not already answer for a driven vehicle a driver drives one truck at a time (a tedder does not count: it goes with its head)
He is not already the new driver on another handover or plan no one is promised two vehicles
He is not being released himself he is leaving
He is in your department a driver change stays inside the department
The documents register holds his valid driving licence, of a class that fits the vehicle (heavy transport for a trailer), and the vehicle's papers (istimara, insurance, periodic inspection, operation card, tank licence) the vehicle cannot legally change hands without them

Steps

  1. Open Vehicle Custody → Driver Change and click New.

  2. Choose the Vehicle. Previous User fills itself in with its current custodian.

  3. Choose the New User from the list, pick the Reason for User Change and add a note if needed.

  4. Click Confirm Driver Change, then confirm. If everything is in order the form moves to Approved and the vehicle is now in the new driver's custody.

  5. Print HR-12, have it signed, scan it, attach the scan under Signed Copy and click Mark as Signed. Your Home page reminds you until you do.

When the documents are missing

If the register lacks a document, the form is refused with the list of what is missing, and a Request Them from HR button:

Click Request Them from HR: one request per missing document goes to HR, and you follow them in My Document Requests (see Missing documents: asking HR). When HR registers them you are told; confirm the driver change again.

If you already asked, the message says so instead, naming the request:

A licence of the wrong class (a private licence for a trailer) is refused without the button: HR cannot fix that by registering it. Choose another driver.

What happens next

  • HR gets a message that the change is done, with anything the register is missing for the vehicle, for information.
  • Accounting gets a copy (financial matters, depreciation value).
  • The custody history (Vehicle Custody → Who Answers for What) shows the new driver from today.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
A driver is not in the New User list He already holds a vehicle, is promised one, is being released, or is not in your department Hand his vehicle over first, or choose another driver
"… already answers for …, as many vehicles as he may hold" He holds a vehicle Same as above. HR can allow a second vehicle for a recorded reason
"… already has HR12/… open" The vehicle has another open form Finish or Cancel that one first
I started one by mistake It is still a draft Cancel (top left)

Releasing and Requesting Employees

Who is this for: Department managers (Employee Transfers, Requester) How long it takes: 3 minutes per request

What this does

Works like vehicles: your department releases employees it no longer needs, or requests employees it needs (job and number). Only the General Manager decides: he transfers a released employee to a department that asked, holds him, ends his service, or refuses the release. HR then carries out the decision.

Before you start

  • You need Employee Transfers → Requester. You act for the departments you manage (and their sub-departments).
  • If an employee you release answers for vehicles, you also say what happens to each vehicle if he leaves.

Releasing employees

  1. Open Employee Transfers → Requests → Release Employees and click New.

  2. Write the reason, and under Employees to Release add each employee with Why (what the GM should know about him).

  3. Save. If an employee answers for vehicles, a Vehicles in Their Custody tab appears with one line per vehicle. For each, choose If He Leaves:

    If He Leaves What happens when he leaves
    Another Driver in Our Department choose the New Driver; the vehicle stays with you (a driver change)
    Release to the Workshop a vehicle release goes to the GM

    The New Driver list follows the rules in Changing a vehicle's driver: one vehicle per driver, not someone who is leaving, valid licence. A missing document stops the submit with Request Them from HR.

  4. Click Submit to the GM. The request is Submitted and the GM sees the vehicle plan with it.

Nothing happens to the vehicles before he actually leaves. If the GM keeps him, the plan is dropped; if he transfers or his service ends, the plan runs that day.

Requesting employees

  1. Open Employee Transfers → Requests → Request Employees and click New.
  2. Add a line per job: the Job Position, how many, and a note.
  3. Click Submit to the GM.

What happens next

  • The GM decides each employee. A transfer goes to HR, who sets his new manager and approvers and applies it on the effective date. If he still answers for a vehicle, the transfer waits until the vehicle is handed over.
  • For a request, the GM may give you released employees, or have HR hire from outside.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
"… answers for TRN-…: say in Vehicles in Their Custody what happens to it" A vehicle has no plan Open the tab and choose If He Leaves
An employee is not in the list He is not in a department you manage, or is already released Ask HR to check his department
The New Driver I want is not offered He holds a vehicle, is leaving, or is promised one See Changing a vehicle's driver

Missing Documents: Asking HR

Who is this for: Department supervisors How long it takes: 1 minute

What this does

A vehicle cannot change hands while the documents register lacks one of its papers or the new driver's licence. HR keeps the register; you do not see it. Instead, the message that stops you carries a Request Them from HR button, and each missing document becomes a Document Request (DR/2026/…) that is yours to follow.

Steps

  1. Click Request Them from HR on the message (see Changing a vehicle's driver). A green note confirms the request numbers.

  2. Follow them in Employee Transfers → Requests → My Document Requests, or on the release's Vehicles in Their Custody tab (Document Requests column).

  3. Open one to see what was asked, why, and its state.

State Means
Waiting for HR HR has it on their Home page
Registered HR has registered the document; you got a message. Submit or confirm again
Cancelled You or HR cancelled it

What happens next

The request closes itself the moment a valid document is in the register, whether HR typed it from your request or it came in with a government export. You do not need to chase it: you are told.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
"[HR has DR/… since …]" in the message You (or another supervisor) already asked Wait for the message that it is registered
I asked by mistake It is still waiting Open it and click Cancel Request

Issuing a Workshop Service Request

Who is this for: Workshop Requesters: the Isuzu and trailer supervisors, and the maintenance department manager How long it takes: 2 minutes

What this does

Asks the workshop to inspect or repair one of your vehicles, as a tracked request instead of a phone call or the old Google Form. The workshop manager is notified the moment you submit. You can follow the request from the same screen until the vehicle is done.

Each request gets a number (WS00001, WS00002, …) and moves through four states:

State What it means
New You have submitted it; the workshop has not started yet. You can still correct it.
In Progress The workshop accepted it and is working on the vehicle.
Completed The work is done. You get a message, often with collection details.
Rejected The workshop will not do this request. You get a message explaining why.

Before you start

  • You need the Workshop Requester role. Only the people who answer for vehicles raise requests. If you open Workshop → My Requests and there is no New button, you don't have the role. Ask your system administrator. (Drivers and other staff report a fault to their supervisor, who raises the request.)

  • You can only pick your own vehicles. The vehicle list offers:

    • the trucks in your tanker groups, and
    • the trucks driven by your team: anyone in your reporting line, and you.

    The tanker groups are the same ones you use in Water Delivery → Tankers. If a truck you are responsible for is missing, it has to be added to your tanker group first.

  • Know the vehicle's fleet number (e.g. IS042, T171) and be ready to describe the problem: what is wrong, since when, and any warning light or noise.

Steps

  1. Open the Workshop app, then My Requests. This list shows every request you have raised and its current state.

  2. Click New. A blank request opens. Requested By is you and cannot be changed. Client is already set to the company, which is right for any company vehicle.

  3. Choose the Vehicle Type: Trailer, Isuzu, Tank or Other. Choose it first, because the Vehicle list only shows vehicles of that type.

  4. Pick the Vehicle. Click the field or start typing the fleet number. Only your own trucks of that type are listed: the ones in your tanker groups and the ones your team drives.

  5. Check the Driver. When you pick the truck, Driver fills itself in with the driver attached to it. That is normally the person BAS shows driving it most over the last two weeks, or its default driver. If someone else is bringing the truck in, change it. This is allowed, but the request will then say so (see What happens next). Driver is required.

  6. Write the Description. This is the most important field. The workshop reads it to plan the job, and the system uses it to sort the request by repair type (tyres, brakes, electrical…), so you do not choose a type yourself. Write plainly. For example, write "Engine overheats on long trips; the temperature needle goes into the red after about an hour", not just "engine problem".

  7. Click Submit Request (top left). The request is saved, gets its WS… number and is sent to the workshop. A green Request submitted message confirms it. (Saving with the cloud icon does exactly the same thing.)

What happens next

  • It goes to your department's workshop: Nabiya, Tabuk or Al-Hayat, whichever serves your department (Nabiya if none). That workshop's manager is told.
  • The workshop manager reviews it. When they accept it, the state moves to In Progress and a technician starts on the vehicle. You can watch the state bar at the top of the request.
  • When the work is finished, the request moves to Completed. You receive a message in your Inbox (the chat icon at the top right), on the request itself. It often includes a note from the workshop, such as where to collect the vehicle.
  • If the workshop will not do it, the request moves to Rejected. The message tells you why. A common reason is that the same vehicle already has an open request.
  • If you changed the driver, a yellow Driver changed box shows at the top of the request for everyone who opens it. It names the driver you entered and the driver attached to the truck, so the workshop knows who really brought it in. This is normal when the regular driver is on leave. Say why in the description.
  • To talk to the workshop about a request, use the message box on the right of the request (the chatter). Click External, type and send. Attach a photo of the damage or warning light the same way. Everything stays on the request instead of in calls.

What you can and cannot change. While the request is New you can correct anything except Requested By: open it, edit, save. Once the workshop has started, the request is locked for you. To add information, write in the chatter. The repair report (entry and exit times, odometer, parts, labour) is filled in by the workshop.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
There is no New button on My Requests You don't have the Workshop Requester role Ask your system administrator. Until then, ask a supervisor who has it
The Vehicle list is empty Vehicle Type is not set, or none of your trucks is of that type Choose the right type first; the list follows it
A truck I am responsible for is not in the list It is not in your tanker groups, and nobody on your team is its driver Ask for it to be added to your tanker group (Water Delivery → Tankers)
"… is not one of your vehicles: it is not in your tanker groups and none of your team drives it" The truck was picked some other way but is not yours Pick one of your own trucks, or have it added to your tanker group
Driver filled in with the wrong person BAS shows that person driving the truck most recently Change it to whoever is bringing the truck. The request will show Driver changed, which is expected
"Vehicle Type and Driver are required…" when saving Driver is empty (the truck has no driver attached) Pick the driver yourself
I can't edit my request any more It is no longer New: the workshop has started, finished or rejected it Write in the request's chatter instead
There is no Delete option Requests are never deleted, so the history stays complete If you raised it by mistake, ask the workshop in the chatter to reject it

Deciding Vehicle Requests

Who is this for: General Manager (Vehicle Allocation, General Manager) How long it takes: 1 minute per vehicle

What this does

Departments release vehicles and request vehicles; you are the only one who decides. You decide each vehicle, from the request itself. Everything after your decision (the workshop, HR's checks, the handover form) is execution and needs nothing more from you.

Before you start

  • Every request waiting for you is on your Home page and in Vehicle Allocation → GM Decisions → Requests to Decide.

Deciding a release

  1. Open the request and click Decide Vehicles.

  2. Choose the Decision for the vehicles listed:

    Decision Effect
    Give them to a department that asked choose For Request: only open requests of the same type, with room, and a need that fits (with or without driver) are offered. The vehicle goes to the workshop for a checkup, then to that department
    Send to workshop and hold the vehicle goes to the workshop and waits in the Holding Pool for a future request
    Keep them where they are the department keeps it. Write why in the note (they asked to give it up)

    The lower table, Requests These Vehicles Can Fill, shows what is open.

  3. Click Confirm Decision.

Deciding a request for vehicles

  1. Open the request and click Decide on the line.

  2. Choose the Mode:

    Mode Effect
    Give vehicles we already have pick from vehicles other departments released that fit, or the holding pool (checkup completed)
    Obtain outside (purchase / rent) the number to buy or rent; the line waits for them
    Allocate an obtained vehicle that arrived gives a purchased or rented vehicle that has arrived
    Refuse write why

  3. Click Confirm Decision.

What happens next

The departments are told. A vehicle you give goes through the workshop and the HR-12 handover forms; the receiving department names its driver. A vehicle released with its driver moves at once with him.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
For Request offers nothing No open request fits (type, need, quantity, not the same department) Hold it, or wait for a request
A release has no Decide button It is not submitted yet, or already decided Check its state

Deciding Employee Releases and Requests

Who is this for: General Manager (Employee Transfers, General Manager) How long it takes: 1 minute per employee

What this does

Departments release employees and request employees; you decide. For a released employee you also see what his department planned for the vehicles in his custody, because your decision carries that plan out.

Deciding a release

  1. Open the request from your Home page or Employee Transfers → GM Decisions → Released Employees, and click Decide.

  2. Read both tables: the employees, and their vehicles, if they leave (driver change to whom, or release to the workshop).

  3. Choose the Decision:

    Decision Effect
    Transfer to a Request choose For Request (a department that asked for his job) and the Effective Date. HR completes the transfer
    On Hold he stays and is offered to the next request
    End of Service Effective Date = his termination date; HR completes it
    Refuse the Release he stays; nothing happens to his vehicles

  4. Click Confirm. On a transfer or end of service, the vehicle plan runs now: a driver change is completed, a release to the workshop goes to Vehicle Allocation. If something in the plan is no longer valid (an expired licence, a driver taken meanwhile) the decision is stopped with the reason: return it to the department.

Deciding a request for employees

  1. Open the request and click Decide on the line.
  2. Give Released Employees (choose them), Hire from Outside, On Hold or Refuse, with the number.
  3. Click Confirm.

What happens next

HR sets each transferred employee's new manager and approvers and applies the transfer on its date; HR names the hires. The departments are told.

HR-12: Checking a Handover's Documents

Who is this for: HR / Administrative Affairs (Vehicle Custody, HR / Administrative Affairs) How long it takes: 2 minutes per form

What this does

The handover form HR-12 follows a vehicle when it changes hands. HR has a step on two kinds of form; the GM already decided, so you complete your section, you do not approve:

Form When Your part
Hand In (Release) a released vehicle goes to the workshop check its documents are handed in, in parallel with the workshop's inspection
Receive a department receives a vehicle and names its driver check the new driver and the documents before the workshop hands it over

A Driver Change inside a department has no HR step any more: it is done when the supervisor confirms it, and you get a message listing anything the register is missing, for information.

Steps

  1. Open the form from your Home page (HR documents: …) or Vehicle Custody → Handover Forms (HR-12).

  2. Open the HR / Administrative Affairs tab. Each document line shows what the register holds (Valid / Expiring Soon / Expired / Not in the Register) with its expiry, so you do not re-check it by hand. Tick Handed Over for what you received, and add remarks.

  3. On a Receive, the new user's licence type and expiry are read from the register; the driver's checks (one vehicle, his department, a valid licence of the right class) run again.

  4. Click Documents Checked. On a hand-in the form waits for the workshop if it has not finished; on a receipt it goes to the workshop, whose handover is the vehicle's arrival.

What happens next

When the workshop is also done, the form is Approved: the custodian changes, Accounting and the department are told. The department prints, signs and files the paper.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
No Documents Checked button The form is not at your step Check the status bar
"… cannot change hands until the documents register holds …" The register lacks a document of the vehicle or driver Register it (see The documents register), then click again

The Documents Register and Document Requests

Who is this for: HR / Administrative Affairs (Documents, Documents Officer) How long it takes: varies

What this does

The Documents app is the one register of every vehicle's and employee's papers: istimara, insurance, periodic inspection, operation card, tank licence, driving licence, driver card, iqama, passport. Handovers read it: a vehicle cannot change hands while a checked document is missing or expired. Supervisors cannot see the register; when they need something in it, they send you a Document Request.

Answering document requests

  1. Open Documents → Requests (also on your Home page as Document requests). Oldest first; each says what is needed, for whom, why (e.g. New driver of TRN-IS2) and who asked.

  2. Open the request and click Register the Document. The document form opens already pointing at the employee or vehicle.

  3. Fill in the number and expiry (attach the scan if you have it) and Confirm. The request closes itself and the supervisor is told.

Any valid document of the same kind confirmed for that person or vehicle closes the request, however it was entered (upload, bulk upload, export).

Importing government exports

The fastest way to fill the register. Documents → Government Exports → New, choose the Source, attach the file, Preview, check the warnings, then Apply. Nothing is written before Apply; nothing in Odoo is overwritten by an older document.

Source From Fills
Absher Business: Vehicles Absher ▸ Vehicles istimara, inspection, insurance status, actual user
Absher Business: Driving Licence Holders Absher ▸ licence holders (residents) driving licence: number = iqama, class, expiry
Muqeem: Active Residents Muqeem ▸ active residents iqama, passport
Qiwa / GOSI the portals' lists contracts, registration
Tank Inspection Report (Fahes) the inspection report water tank licence

The licence preview also warns about a custodian of a trailer holding a licence of the wrong class, and lists residents who hold a vehicle but have no licence in the file.

What a handover checks

Documents → Configuration → Document Types: the column Checked at Vehicle Handover decides which documents stop a handover. The driver card is off until the cards are registered for everyone; switch it on then.

A driver who may hold two vehicles

One driver holds one driven vehicle. For an approved exception, open the employee, tab Vehicle Custody, set Vehicles He May Hold to 2 and write Why More Than One.

Employee Transfers: HR's Part

Who is this for: HR (Employee Transfers, HR) How long it takes: 3 minutes per transfer

What this does

When the GM transfers a released employee, or ends his service, HR carries it out. You do not decide; you complete what the move needs.

Completing a transfer

  1. Open it from your Home page or Employee Transfers → Transfers. It is With HR.
  2. Check the proposed new manager, time off approver, attendance approver and HR responsible: they come from the new department. Correct them if needed (all required).
  3. Click Confirm. It waits for its effective date (Waiting for Date) and is Applied that day: his department, manager and approvers change.

Vehicles first. If he still answers for a vehicle, the transfer waits until the vehicle is handed over (the driver change or hand-in from his release plan), then applies at once.

If the transfer cannot go ahead, click Return to the GM with the reason.

End of service and hires

  • End of Service decided by the GM: open the release and click End of Service to start his end-of-service request with the termination date.
  • Hire from Outside: when the new employees are created, open the request and click Name New Hires.

The Workshop Manager's Queue, Sites and Handover Forms

Who is this for: Workshop Managers and Technicians How long it takes: ongoing

What this does

There are three workshops: Nabiya (the main one), Tabuk and Al-Hayat. Every request goes to one of them, and you work the queue of your own workshop(s). Besides repair requests, the workshop has a step on the vehicle handover forms (HR-12): inspecting a vehicle that is handed in, and handing a vehicle over to its new department.

Before you start

  • You are in your workshop's Managers & Staff (Workshop → Configuration → Workshops). In none, you see nothing: ask your administrator.
  • The GM and the reviewers see every workshop; on the dashboard they switch between them.

Where a request goes

A request goes to the workshop that serves the requester's department (or its nearest parent department), otherwise to Nabiya. Requests raised before the three workshops existed are Nabiya's.

  1. Open Workshop → All Requests. The Workshop column shows the site; you only see yours.

  2. Work the requests as before: accept (In Progress), fill the repair report, Complete or Reject. See the requester's guide, Issuing a workshop service request, for what they see.

Handover forms (HR-12)

Open them from your Home page (Workshop: TRN-…) or Vehicle Custody → Handover Forms (HR-12).

Form Your part
Hand In (Release) inspect the vehicle that is handed in, in parallel with HR's documents check. A checkup Workshop Request is opened with it (button at the top)
Receive after HR has checked the new driver, hand the vehicle over to him: that is its arrival in the new department

  1. Open the Vehicle Inspection tab. Fill Inspected On and, per item (clutch, tyres, brakes…), the Depreciation %, Value and a remark. The Total Value is what Accounting receives.

  2. Click Workshop Done. On a hand-in the form is Approved when HR has also checked the documents; on a receipt it is the arrival: the vehicle is the department's and the driver's from now on.

A driver change between two drivers of the same department has no workshop step any more.

Setting Up Vehicles, Custody, Transfers and Documents

Who is this for: the system administrator How long it takes: one sitting at go-live, then as people change

What this does

The apps need four kinds of setup: who answers for which department, which departments hold vehicles and which workshop serves them, who works in which workshop, and what a handover checks. Do them in this order at go-live.

1. Vehicle-holding departments

Vehicle Allocation → Configuration → Vehicle Departments. Tick Holds Vehicles for the departments that answer for vehicles; Vehicle Count shows how many each has. Every company vehicle needs a department: Vehicle Allocation → Vehicles, filter No department.

2. Who answers for which department

Vehicle Allocation → Configuration → Department Users. Give each supervisor the Department User role and his department(s). The GM gets General Manager, which sees and decides every department.

For Employee Transfers, a Requester acts for the departments he manages (the department's Manager): keep the managers correct in Employees → Departments.

3. Workshops: staff and the departments they serve

Workshop → Configuration → Workshops. For each workshop, add its Managers & Staff, and in Departments Served the departments whose requests it receives (a sub-department follows its parent; a department served by none goes to Nabiya, the main workshop). Someone in no workshop sees no queue.

4. What a handover checks

Documents → Configuration → Document Types, column Checked at Vehicle Handover. A required document of the vehicle or the new driver that is missing or expired stops a handover. Switch a type on only when the register holds it for everyone (import first), or every handover stops.

Rules behind the grant

  • Scope is data, not a role. A Department User acts only for the departments named on him; a Transfer Requester only for the departments he manages. Changing a department's manager changes who can release its employees.
  • One home per decision. Only the GM decides; nobody else holds a decide button. HR and the workshop complete their section; supervisors ask.
  • Least privilege. Nobody receives a role "to be safe": a missing role shows as a missing menu, and is granted when it is needed.
  • The register stays with HR. Supervisors never read employees' documents; they follow their own Document Requests.
