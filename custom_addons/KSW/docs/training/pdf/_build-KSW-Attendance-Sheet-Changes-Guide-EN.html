What Changed on the Attendance Sheet

Who is this for: Supervisors / Direct Managers In effect since: 1 September 2026 Reading time: 3 minutes

The one sentence that matters

The month no longer goes to payroll by itself. You must press ✓ Confirm & Send to Payroll before the month ends — and a month nobody confirms is paid as zero attendance.

What is different

Before Now
The sheet closed and was sent to payroll automatically at month end Nothing reaches payroll until you press ✓ Confirm & Send to Payroll
A month nobody opened was still paid as attended An unconfirmed month is read as zero attendance — the employee is paid for no working days
A sheet could be confirmed even when it contradicted an approved leave Confirmation is refused while a day is marked Attended inside an approved time off, or while a vacation return is still unconfirmed
A leave approved after you finished changed nothing on the sheet A later leave withdraws your confirmation: the sheet returns to Draft and you are asked to confirm it again
The list showed sheets beyond your own team You see only the employees you manage. Sheets needing action sort to the top in red
Friday / Saturday rest days were part of what you marked Rest days are now calculated by the system and cannot be toggled by hand

New on the screen: the ⚡ Apply Approved Time Off button, the ⚠ Needs Attention and Waiting On Me filters, a Waiting On column, and a reminder message on the last day of the month.

What you have to do every month

  1. Open Attendance → Attendance Sheets. The list opens on this year's sheets that have not been sent yet.
  2. Mark the days: ✓ All Present or ✗ All Absent, then correct the exceptions. Rest days and leave-covered days fill themselves.
  3. If the red banner appears on a sheet, clear it — see the next section.
  4. Press ✓ Confirm & Send to Payroll.
    • To do the whole team at once: press the same button in the list header. With no rows ticked it confirms all of your unconfirmed sheets for this month; tick rows first to confirm only those.
  5. The Payroll column turns green and reads Sent to Payroll. Done.

The deadline is the end of the month. A supervisor can only act on the current month. Once the month closes, only HR / the Attendance Sheet Manager can confirm it for you — and until they do, that employee is paid zero days.

The red banner — "This month cannot be sent to payroll yet"

The banner names the reason and, under Waiting on, the person who has to act. There are two reasons:

What it says What to do
N day(s) are marked Attended while <leave type> is approved Press ⚡ Apply Approved Time Off. It marks exactly the days covered by the approved request as absent, to match the request. It changes nothing else.
ON VACATION — <employee> has not been marked as returned You cannot clear this from the sheet. The named Time Off manager must open the request and press Confirm Return. Until then payroll cannot process this employee at all.

If a sheet you already sent comes back

When a time-off request is approved for a day you had already confirmed, the system withdraws the confirmation: the sheet goes back to Draft, its history shows ↩ Confirmation Withdrawn with the reason, and you get a message. Open it, check the days, and press ✓ Confirm & Send to Payroll again. Until you do, that month is read as zero attendance.

Common issues

Symptom Cause Fix
No Confirm & Send to Payroll button on the sheet You are not the employee's direct manager, the month has closed, or the sheet is already confirmed Check the Manager field on the sheet; for a closed month ask HR
The header button says you have nothing to confirm Everything of yours is already sent, or no employee has you as their Manager The message tells you which of the two it is
A day will not toggle It is a Friday/Saturday rest day (calculated), or it is covered by an approved leave Expected — leave it alone
The sheet went back to Draft on its own A leave was approved for a day you had confirmed Re-confirm it
"N Sent to Payroll — M Refused" after a bulk confirm The refused sheets still contradict approved time off The confirmed ones are kept; fix the listed ones and confirm them
The employee was paid zero days The month was never confirmed HR / the Attendance Sheet Manager can still confirm a closed month
