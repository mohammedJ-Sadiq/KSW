# Administrator Guides

For staff who **configure and oversee** whole areas of the system rather than
process individual requests.

> Start with the **General guide** ([Getting Started](../00-getting-started.md)).

## What you can do

| Your area | Guide |
|---|---|
| Oversee all deductions & configure deduction types | [Deductions administration](deductions.md) |
| Configure commissions (categories, sites, rules, salespeople) | [Commissions administration](commissions.md) |

## The golden rules

- Configuration changes affect **everyone** — change catalogs (types,
  categories, rules) deliberately.
- Deduction officers/managers can see and act across the company; keep to your
  area of responsibility.
