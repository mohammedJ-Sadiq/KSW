# Supervisor Guides

For **direct managers** — anyone who has staff reporting to them. You are the
**first approval step** for your team's requests, and you maintain their
attendance and commission sheets.

> You also do everything an [Employee](../employee/) does for yourself. Read
> those guides too. And start with the **General guide** ([Getting Started](../00-getting-started.md)).

## What you can do

| I want to… | Guide |

## The golden rules

- You see and act on **yourself + your direct reports**.
- You are the **Direct Manager (DM)** step — the **first** approval on both
  leave and loan requests. After you approve, it goes to HR.
- **Time-off** requests live in **Time Off → Management → Time Off**;
  **loan** requests live in **Loans → Operations → Loans**.
- Every approval/refusal is recorded with your name; a refusal always needs a
  **reason**, which the employee sees.
