# Accounting Guides

For **Accounting staff**. You approve loans and leave at the **Accounting step**,
confirm loan **disbursement**, create **penalties**, and **finalise** commission
sheets into payment batches.

> You also do everything an [Employee](../employee/) does. Start with
> the **General guide** ([Getting Started](../00-getting-started.md)).

## What you can do

| I want to… | Guide |
|---|---|
| Approve leave at the Accounting step | [Leave: Accounting approval](01-leave-acc-approval.md) |
| Approve a loan and confirm disbursement | [Loan: approval & disbursement](02-loan-approval-disbursement.md) |
| Record a government or internal penalty | [Create a penalty](05-create-penalties.md) |
| Finalise commission sheets & close the batch | [Commissions: finalise & batch](04-commission-finalise-batch.md) |

## The golden rules

- You are the **Accounting** step on both loans and annual leave — you check the
  financial impact (remaining loans, commissions, flight tickets).
- **Disbursement** is the final gate that turns an approved loan **Active** so
  its installments start.
- **Penalties** (government + internal) are yours to create. **Salary advances**
  and **all installment changes** are handled by **HR**.
- The **bank file export** (salaries and commissions) is performed by **HR**,
  not Accounting — you finalise and close the batch; HR runs the export.
- **Loans** live in **Loans → Operations → Loans** (opens on **Waiting For
  Me**); **time-off** approvals live in **Time Off → Management → Time Off**.
