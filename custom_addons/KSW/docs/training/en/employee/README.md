# Employee Guides

For **every staff member**. These cover the things you do for yourself, no
matter your department. You only ever see and act on your **own** records.

> First time in the system? Read the **General guide** ([Getting Started](../00-getting-started.md)) first.

## What you can do

| I want to… | Guide |
|---|---|
| See my attendance and whether a day is covered | [View my attendance](01-view-attendance.md) |
| Request annual, sick, unpaid or other leave | [Request time off](02-request-time-off.md) |
| Ask for a loan and track its approval | [Request a loan](03-request-a-loan.md) |
| View and download my payslip | [View my payslip](04-view-payslip.md) |
| Report an IT problem or ask IT for something | [Raise an IT support ticket](05-raise-support-ticket.md) |

## The golden rules

- **You act only on your own records.** Requests you create go to your **direct
  manager** first, then onward through the approval chain.
- **Watch the status bar** on any request to see where it is.
- **Use your Inbox** (bell icon, top-right) — you are notified at every step,
  including if a request is refused and why.
